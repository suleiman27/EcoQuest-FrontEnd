Maasai Mara Safari – 3 Days

Day 1 – Arrival & Game Drive

Depart from your hotel in the morning and travel to the Maasai Mara National Reserve.

Enjoy lunch followed by an afternoon game drive through the reserve. Explore the spectacular savannah landscapes and search for lions, elephants, buffaloes, leopards, cheetahs, giraffes and other wildlife.

Return to your lodge or camp for dinner and an overnight stay.

Day 2 – Full Day Maasai Mara Safari

After breakfast, enjoy a full-day game drive in the Maasai Mara.

The day provides an opportunity to explore different parts of the reserve while searching for the Big Five and other wildlife.

Enjoy a picnic lunch in the reserve before continuing your game drive.

Return to your lodge or camp in the evening for dinner and overnight accommodation.

Day 3 – Morning Game Drive & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast. This is an excellent time to experience the Mara while wildlife is active.

Return for breakfast, check out and begin your journey back to your hotel.

What to Expect

  • Maasai Mara game drives
  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • Beautiful savannah landscapes
  • Professional safari guide
  • Comfortable accommodation
  • Excellent photography opportunities
