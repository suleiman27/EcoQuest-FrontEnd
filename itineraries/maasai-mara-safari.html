Maasai Mara Safari

3 Days / 2 Nights

Day 1 – Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to the Maasai Mara. Enjoy an afternoon game drive across the reserve, discovering its spectacular landscapes and abundant wildlife. Dinner and overnight at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Full-Day Maasai Mara Safari

Enjoy a full day exploring the Maasai Mara, with excellent opportunities to spot the Big Five, diverse wildlife and numerous bird species. Enjoy a picnic lunch in the reserve before continuing your safari.

Day 3 – Morning Game Drive & Departure

Early morning game drive before breakfast, offering a chance to experience the Mara at its most active. After breakfast, depart for Nairobi or your chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

Big five, vast savannahs, diverse wildlife and bird species, the Mara River, and the spectacular Great Migration seasonally.
