3 Days Safari

Tsavo West & Ngutuni – 3 Days

Day 1 – Tsavo West

Depart from your hotel and travel to Tsavo West National Park. Enjoy a game drive upon arrival, exploring the park's spectacular volcanic landscapes and diverse wildlife.

Enjoy lunch followed by an afternoon game drive before returning to your lodge or camp for dinner and an overnight stay.

Day 2 – Ngutuni Wildlife Conservancy

After breakfast, continue to the Ngutuni area and enjoy a wildlife experience surrounded by scenic landscapes.

Explore the conservancy during game drives and look out for elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and other wildlife.

Depending on the safari schedule, enjoy an evening or night game drive before returning for dinner and overnight accommodation.

Day 3 – Morning Game Drive & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast. Take in the final wildlife sightings and beautiful scenery before checking out.

Begin your journey back to your hotel after the morning safari.

What to Expect

  • Tsavo West game drives
  • Ngutuni wildlife experience
  • Volcanic landscapes
  • Opportunities for wildlife photography
  • Professional safari guide
  • Comfortable accommodation
