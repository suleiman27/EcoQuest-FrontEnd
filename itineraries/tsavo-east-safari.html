Tsavo East Safari – 2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Arrival & Game Drive

Pick-up from your hotel in the morning and departure to Tsavo East National Park. Enjoy a game drive upon arrival, followed by lunch and an afternoon game drive. Discover the park’s spectacular landscapes and abundant wildlife as you explore one of Kenya’s largest and most famous national parks.

Tsavo East is renowned for its vast open plains, diverse wildlife and the famous red elephants. During the game drive, keep an eye out for elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, antelopes and many species of birds.

In the evening, return to your lodge or camp for dinner and an overnight stay.

Day 2 – Morning Game Drive & Departure

Wake up early for a morning game drive, when wildlife is most active and the temperatures are cooler.

Enjoy the opportunity to see more wildlife and experience the beautiful scenery of Tsavo East as the sun rises over the park.

Return to the lodge for breakfast before checking out and beginning your journey back to your hotel.

Depending on your schedule, you may enjoy a final game drive before leaving the park.

Safari Experience

This two-day Tsavo East safari offers an exciting introduction to Kenya's wildlife and natural beauty. From the dramatic landscapes to unforgettable wildlife encounters, every moment provides an opportunity to experience the wild side of Kenya.

What to Expect

  • Morning and afternoon game drives
  • Opportunities to see elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras and buffaloes
  • Spectacular Tsavo East landscapes
  • Comfortable lodge or camp accommodation
  • Professional safari guide
  • Memorable wildlife experiences
