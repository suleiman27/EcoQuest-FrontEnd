Tsavo East Safari

2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Arrival & Game Drive

Pick-up from your hotel in the morning and departure to Tsavo East National Park. Enjoy a game drive upon arrival, followed by lunch and an afternoon game drive. Discover the park’s spectacular landscapes and diverse wildlife before dinner and overnight at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Sunrise Safari & Departure

Start the day with an early morning game drive, offering excellent opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography. After breakfast, enjoy a final game drive before departing the park and returning to your hotel or chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

Big 4, diverse wildlife, spectacular landscapes, iconic red elephants and a rich variety of bird species.
