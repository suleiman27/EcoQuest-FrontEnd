Ol Pejeta & Samburu Safari

5 Days / 4 Nights

Day 1 – Nairobi to Ol Pejeta

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to Ol Pejeta Conservancy. Enjoy an afternoon game drive through the conservancy, with opportunities to discover its diverse wildlife. Dinner and overnight.

Day 2 – Full-Day Ol Pejeta

Full-day safari exploring Ol Pejeta, home to the Big Five and the world’s last two northern white rhinos. Enjoy the conservancy’s beautiful landscapes and rich wildlife. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3 – Ol Pejeta to Samburu

Morning departure for Samburu National Reserve. Enjoy an afternoon game drive, discovering the unique wildlife and landscapes of northern Kenya. Dinner and overnight.

Day 4 – Full-Day Samburu Safari

Full-day exploration of Samburu, with opportunities to see its unique Special Five, diverse wildlife and bird species. Dinner and overnight.

Day 5 – Samburu & Departure

Early morning game drive before breakfast, followed by a final safari experience. Depart Samburu and return to Nairobi or your chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

Big Five, the world’s last two northern white rhinos, Ol Pejeta’s diverse wildlife, Samburu’s Special Five, spectacular northern landscapes and rich birdlife.
