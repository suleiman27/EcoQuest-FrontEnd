3 Days Safari

Tsavo East & Taita Hills – 3 Days

Day 1 – Tsavo East

Depart from your hotel in the morning and travel to Tsavo East National Park. Enjoy a game drive upon arrival as you explore the park's vast plains and spectacular wildlife.

Look out for elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and many other species. Enjoy lunch before an afternoon game drive.

Dinner and overnight stay at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Taita Hills

After breakfast, depart towards the Taita Hills wildlife area. Enjoy a game drive through the beautiful landscapes and wildlife-rich surroundings.

The area provides excellent opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography.

Dinner and overnight stay at your lodge or camp.

Day 3 – Morning Game Drive & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast. Take in the final views of the wildlife and scenery before checking out.

Begin your journey back to your hotel after the morning safari.

What to Expect

  • Tsavo East game drives
  • Taita Hills wildlife experience
  • Opportunities to see elephants and other wildlife
  • Professional safari guide
  • Comfortable accommodation
  • Scenic landscapes
