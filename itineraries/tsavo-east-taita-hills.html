Tsavo East & Taita Hills Safari

2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Tsavo East & Taita Hills

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to Tsavo East National Park. Enjoy a game drive through the park, followed by lunch before continuing towards Taita Hills for an afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Taita Hills & Departure

Early morning game drive in Taita Hills, offering excellent wildlife and photography opportunities. After breakfast, enjoy a final game drive before departing and returning to your hotel or chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

Diverse wildlife, spectacular landscapes and a variety of bird species across Tsavo East and Taita Hills.
