Tsavo West & Amboseli – 3 Days

Day 1 – Tsavo West

Depart from your hotel in the morning and travel to Tsavo West National Park. Enjoy a game drive upon arrival as you begin exploring the spectacular landscapes and wildlife of the park.

Tsavo West is famous for its volcanic landscapes, wildlife and beautiful scenery. Enjoy lunch followed by an afternoon game drive before returning to your lodge or camp for dinner and an overnight stay.

Day 2 – Amboseli National Park

After breakfast, depart for Amboseli National Park. Enjoy a game drive while exploring the park's open plains and searching for elephants, lions, zebras, giraffes and other wildlife.

On clear days, enjoy spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro, one of Africa's most iconic landmarks.

Dinner and overnight stay at your lodge or camp.

Day 3 – Morning Game Drive & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive in Amboseli before returning for breakfast.

After breakfast, check out and begin your journey back to your hotel.

What to Expect

  • Game drives in Tsavo West
  • Game drives in Amboseli
  • Elephant sightings
  • Mount Kilimanjaro views
  • Professional safari guide
  • Comfortable accommodation
