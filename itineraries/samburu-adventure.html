Samburu Adventure – 3 Days

Day 1 – Arrival & Game Drive

Depart from your hotel and travel north towards Samburu National Reserve.

Enjoy a game drive upon arrival and discover the beautiful landscapes and unique wildlife of northern Kenya.

Dinner and overnight stay at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Full Day Samburu Adventure

Enjoy a full day exploring Samburu National Reserve. The reserve is known for its distinctive wildlife and spectacular northern Kenya scenery.

Look out for the famous Samburu Special Five: the Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, Somali ostrich, gerenuk and Beisa oryx.

Enjoy game drives throughout the day before returning to your lodge or camp for dinner and overnight accommodation.

Day 3 – Morning Game Drive & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast. Take in the final wildlife sightings and landscapes of Samburu.

Return for breakfast, check out and begin your journey back to your hotel.

What to Expect

  • Samburu National Reserve game drives
  • Samburu Special Five
  • Unique northern Kenya landscapes
  • Professional safari guide
  • Comfortable accommodation
  • Wildlife photography opportunities
