Maasai Mara & Lake Nakuru Safari

4 Days / 3 Nights

Day 1 – Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to the Maasai Mara. Enjoy an afternoon game drive before dinner and overnight at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Full-Day Maasai Mara

Spend a full day exploring the reserve, with opportunities to see the Big Five, diverse wildlife and numerous bird species. Picnic lunch in the reserve before continuing the safari.

Day 3 – Maasai Mara to Lake Nakuru

Enjoy an early morning game drive before departing for Lake Nakuru National Park. Arrive for an afternoon game drive, exploring its scenic landscapes, wildlife and rich birdlife. Dinner and overnight.

Day 4 – Lake Nakuru & Departure

Enjoy a morning game drive before departing the park and returning to Nairobi or your chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

The Big Five, vast Mara savannahs, diverse wildlife and bird species, the Mara River, seasonal Great Migration, and the scenic beauty of Lake Nakuru.
