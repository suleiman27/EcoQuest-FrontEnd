Tsavo East Safari & Diani Beach

6 Days / 5 Nights

Tsavo East Safari – 2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Arrival & Game Drive

Pick-up from your hotel in the morning and departure to Tsavo East National Park. Enjoy a game drive upon arrival, followed by lunch and an afternoon game drive. Discover the park’s spectacular landscapes and diverse wildlife before dinner and overnight at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Sunrise Safari & Departure

Start the day with an early morning game drive, offering excellent opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography. After breakfast, enjoy a final game drive before departing the park and continuing to Diani Beach.

Highlights

Big Five, diverse wildlife, spectacular landscapes, iconic red elephants and a rich variety of bird species.

Diani Beach – 4 Days / 3 Nights

Day 3 – Arrival & Beach Relaxation

Arrival in Diani Beach and check-in at your beach resort. Spend the rest of the day relaxing on the beautiful white sandy beach and enjoying the warm waters of the Indian Ocean.

Day 4 – Beach Day

Enjoy a full day at leisure on the beautiful beaches of Diani. Relax, swim, sunbathe or simply enjoy the peaceful tropical atmosphere.

Day 5 – Beach Day

Another relaxing day by the Indian Ocean. Enjoy the beach and your resort at your own pace.

Day 6 – Final Morning & Departure

Enjoy your final morning at the beach before checking out. Transfer to your chosen drop-off point, such as Mombasa Airport, the SGR station or your next hotel.

Diani Beach Highlights

White sandy beaches, warm Indian Ocean waters, palm-fringed coastline and a peaceful tropical atmosphere.

Optional Activities

Guests interested in additional experiences such as dhow cruises, snorkeling, dolphin watching, diving, kitesurfing or other water activities can arrange them at an additional cost.
