Tsavo East, Taita Hills & Diani Beach

6 Days / 5 Nights

Tsavo East & Taita Hills Safari – 2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Tsavo East & Taita Hills

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to Tsavo East National Park. Enjoy a game drive through the park, followed by lunch before continuing towards Taita Hills for an afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Taita Hills & Departure

Early morning game drive in Taita Hills, offering excellent wildlife and photography opportunities. After breakfast, enjoy a final game drive before departing and continuing towards Diani Beach.

Highlights

Big Five, diverse wildlife, spectacular landscapes and a variety of bird species across Tsavo East and Taita Hills.

Diani Beach – 4 Days / 3 Nights

Day 3 – Arrival & Beach Relaxation

Arrival in Diani Beach and check-in at your beach resort. Spend the rest of the day relaxing on the beautiful white sandy beach and enjoying the warm waters of the Indian Ocean.

Day 4 – Beach Day

Enjoy a full day at leisure on the beautiful beaches of Diani. Relax, swim, sunbathe or simply enjoy the peaceful tropical atmosphere.

Day 5 – Beach Day

Another relaxing day by the Indian Ocean. Enjoy the beach and your resort at your own pace.

Day 6 – Final Morning & Departure

Enjoy your final morning at the beach before checking out. Transfer to your chosen drop-off point, such as Mombasa Airport, the SGR station or your next hotel.

Diani Beach Highlights

White sandy beaches, warm Indian Ocean waters, palm-fringed coastline and a peaceful tropical atmosphere.

Optional Activities

Dhow cruises, snorkeling, dolphin watching, diving, kitesurfing and other water activities can be arranged upon request at an additional cost.
