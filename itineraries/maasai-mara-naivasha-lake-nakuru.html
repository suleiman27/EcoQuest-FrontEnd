Maasai Mara, Naivasha & Lake Nakuru Safari

5 Days / 4 Nights

Day 1 – Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to the Maasai Mara. Enjoy an afternoon game drive through the reserve. Dinner and overnight.

Day 2 – Full-Day Maasai Mara

Full-day safari exploring the Mara’s vast savannahs, with opportunities to see the Big Five, diverse wildlife and bird species. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3 – Maasai Mara to Lake Naivasha

Morning departure for Lake Naivasha. Enjoy a relaxing boat ride on the lake, surrounded by beautiful scenery and abundant birdlife. Dinner and overnight.

Day 4 – Naivasha to Lake Nakuru

Depart for Lake Nakuru National Park and enjoy a game drive through its scenic landscapes, with opportunities to spot diverse wildlife and bird species. Dinner and overnight.

Day 5 – Lake Nakuru & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive before departing the park and returning to Nairobi or your chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

The Big Five, Maasai Mara’s vast savannahs, Lake Naivasha boat ride, diverse wildlife and birdlife, scenic Great Rift Valley landscapes and Lake Nakuru National Park.
