Amboseli & Tsavo East Safari

3 Days / 2 Nights

Day 1 – Amboseli National Park

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to Amboseli. Enjoy a game drive with spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro, while exploring the park’s famous elephant herds and diverse wildlife. Dinner and overnight at your lodge or camp.

Day 2 – Amboseli to Tsavo East

Enjoy an early morning game drive in Amboseli before departing for Tsavo East. Arrive for an afternoon game drive and discover its vast landscapes, abundant wildlife and rich birdlife. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3 – Tsavo East & Departure

Early morning game drive in search of elephants, lions, buffaloes and other wildlife. After breakfast, enjoy a final game drive before departing the park and returning to your hotel or chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

Majestic Mount Kilimanjaro, Amboseli’s iconic elephant herds, diverse wildlife, spectacular savannah landscapes and a rich variety of bird species.
