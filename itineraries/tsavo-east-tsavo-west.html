Tsavo East & Tsavo West Safari

3 Days / 2 Nights

Day 1 – Tsavo East

Morning pick-up from your hotel and departure to Tsavo East. Enjoy game drives through the park, discovering its vast landscapes, diverse wildlife and iconic red elephants. Dinner and overnight.

Day 2 – Tsavo East to Tsavo West

Early morning game drive before departing for Tsavo West National Park. Continue with an afternoon game drive, exploring the park’s dramatic scenery and rich wildlife. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3 – Tsavo West & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive, with opportunities to discover wildlife and visit the beautiful Mzima Springs before departing the park and returning to your hotel or chosen drop-off point.

Highlights

Tsavo’s famous red elephants, Big Five, diverse wildlife and bird species, spectacular landscapes, volcanic scenery and the crystal-clear waters of Mzima Springs.
